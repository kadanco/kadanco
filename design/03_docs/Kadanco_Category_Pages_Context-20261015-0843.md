## Style Rules (locked)
- 5px radius; buttons/controls 45px tall.
- Font weights 400 and 500 only; no bold paragraph text.
- No glass effects.
- Labels outlined, not filled.
- Headlines Title Case, two sentences separated by a full stop; CTAs Title Case.
- Dept colours: Product Blue #3B38DF · Marketing Neon Yellow #DAFF02 (dark-blue text, never large bg) · Sales Purple #B2B1FF · PLG Orange #FE572A.
- Imagery: 3D isometric icons; placeholder captions double as a shot list.

## Open / placeholder items
- All metrics and quotes in case studies and before/after are invented — **review before launch**.
- Image placeholders (~25) need real photos.
- CTAs and next-floor links are dummy (`#`); no working form yet.

## Security notes (for build)
- Contact form: server-side validation, spam protection, rate limiting.
- GDPR (Germany): real Imprint + Privacy, cookie consent before analytics, consent-gated map.
- Self-host fonts (Google Fonts = GDPR risk in Germany).
- HTTPS + security headers; double opt-in newsletter.

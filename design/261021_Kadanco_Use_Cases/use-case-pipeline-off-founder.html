Sales · Founder-led sales

Pipeline that runs without the founder in every deal.

A sales process that books meetings without the founder in every deal. We build the motion, the outbound engine and the enablement so pipeline keeps moving when the founder steps back.

Get a free auditBook a Strategy Call
The situation

Every deal needed the founder. So pipeline capped out.

The founder could sell, but nobody else could, so growth was limited to the hours the founder could spend in calls.

The problem

No repeatable process, no outbound engine, and knowledge locked in the founder's head. Pipeline stopped the moment the founder got pulled into the product.

What we do

We turn how the founder sells into a documented, repeatable motion, build an outbound engine around it, and enable the team so meetings get booked without the founder in every thread.

What's inside

A sales motion that runs without you.

Three parts that take pipeline off the founder.

01 · Repeatable process

The founder motion, written down

We capture how the founder qualifies, pitches and closes, and turn it into a process anyone on the team can follow and improve.

02 · Outbound engine

Meetings booked on purpose

Targeted lists, sequences and messaging that book qualified meetings consistently, so pipeline is built deliberately rather than by referral luck.

03 · Handoff & enablement

A team that can carry it

Scripts, objection handling and training so reps run the motion with confidence and the founder only joins the deals that truly need them.

What you get

A pipeline that does not depend on one person.

Repeatable

A documented motion anyone on the team can run.

Consistent

Outbound that books qualified meetings every week.

Founder-light

The founder joins only the deals that need them.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Will the founder stop selling entirely?

No. The founder stays on the deals where they add most. The goal is to remove them from every deal, not every deal.

Do you do the outbound for us?

We build the engine and can run it or train your team to, depending on where you are.

How fast until meetings land?

First outbound is live within two weeks; booked meetings typically follow within the first cycle.

What about our CRM?

We work in your existing CRM, or set one up if the process needs it, and keep the pipeline measurable.

Tell us how your founder closes.

We'll turn it into a motion your team can run without them.

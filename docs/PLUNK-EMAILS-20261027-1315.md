# Plunk emails for the five forms

Written 27 October 2026. Copy each subject and body into a Plunk template. Variables use Plunk's `{{key}}` syntax; the keys are the fields each form sends with its event (list in the chat of 27 October and in `src/data/intakes.ts`). `{{brief}}` holds every answer as "Label: answer", one per line.

Two things to test once in Plunk before relying on these:

1. Whether event data (for example `{{brief}}`) shows up in a template. Plunk documents `{{key}}` for contact data; send one test brief and preview the email.
2. A workflow's "Send email" step sends to the contact, which is the visitor. That is right for the confirmations. The alerts to us need a fixed address; if Plunk cannot send there, the form handler can send them instead (offered in the chat).

Fallbacks: `{{name ?? 'there'}}` prints "there" when a field is empty.

---

## 1. Strategy call (event `contact-form-submitted`)

Fields: `name`, `company`, `website`, `goal`, plus the contact's `{{email}}`.

### Alert to us

**Subject:** New strategy call request: {{name}} ({{company ?? 'no company'}})

**Body:**

```
A new strategy call request came in.

Name: {{name}}
Email: {{email}}
Company: {{company ?? '-'}}
Website: {{website}}

Problem to solve:
{{goal}}

We promised a reply within one working day.
```

### Confirmation to the visitor (English)

**Subject:** Thanks, {{name ?? 'there'}}. We have your message.

**Body:**

```
Hi {{name ?? 'there'}},

thank you for reaching out. We have your message and reply within one working day to suggest times for a call.

What happens next:
1. We read your message.
2. We reply within one working day and suggest times.
3. We talk it through on a call with the operators who would do the work.

If we are not the right fit, we tell you on the first call.

Want to add something? Just reply to this email.

Maria and Karan
Kadanco
```

### Confirmation to the visitor (German)

**Betreff:** Danke, {{name ?? ''}}. Ihre Nachricht ist angekommen.

**Text:**

```
Guten Tag {{name ?? ''}},

vielen Dank für Ihre Nachricht. Wir melden uns innerhalb eines Werktags und schlagen Termine für ein Gespräch vor.

Wie es weitergeht:
1. Wir lesen Ihre Nachricht.
2. Wir antworten innerhalb eines Werktags und schlagen Termine vor.
3. Wir sprechen alles in einem Gespräch mit den Machern durch, die die Arbeit machen würden.

Wenn wir nicht passen, sagen wir es Ihnen im ersten Gespräch.

Möchten Sie etwas ergänzen? Antworten Sie einfach auf diese E-Mail.

Maria und Karan
Kadanco
```
